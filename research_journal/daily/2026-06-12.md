## Decisions

## LP-FT result (evening) — ❌ FAILED
- LP-FT (pure linear probe, frozen Paddy backbone): **61%** vs OLD full-FT **72%** — 11pp DROP. No Grad-CAM improvement. ~25 min train.
- Why: froze the rice-specialized Paddy backbone (wrong features for PlantDoc's multi-crop diversity); frozen backbone + linear head can't change attention.
- Lesson: feature-preservation tricks (R, LP-FT) both fail because they don't remove clutter. Next = **detect-then-crop** (removes clutter directly; PlantDoc ~30%→70.5% historically).

## Detect-then-crop result (late) — ❌ FAILED
- Detection-repo test gave fake 91.9% = leakage (those imgs were in training). Discarded.
- Honest held-out HF test: no-crop 72.3%, **YOLO-crop 58.2%** (−14pp). Cropping HURTS — removes the background crutch the model depends on.
- Third failed fix (after R, LP-FT). All fail because none retrains the model to look at leaves.

## Conclusion reached today
- 72.3% is the PlantDoc frontier (~74–78% SOTA); no method reliably beats it.
- The 72% is background-driven (rigorously diagnosed).
- Contribution = the IKS advisory SYSTEM + the disease-bias diagnosis (3 documented failed fixes), NOT a SOTA classifier.
